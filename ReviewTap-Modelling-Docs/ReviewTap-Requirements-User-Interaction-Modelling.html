User Roles — Three Kinds of People

Visitor / Customer (no login)Anyone who opens the site or scans a QR: reads info pages, leaves reviews, feedback and complaints
Business Owner (login)One business only: staff, QRs, inbox, attendance, reports, branding, plan view
Platform Admin (login)All businesses: create tenants, plans, global staff + AI views

Quality Promises — Non-Negotiable Behaviors

  • Private stays private: feedback, complaints and internal notes are never posted publicly and never shown to customers.
  • No forced sign-ups: customers never create accounts; names and contacts are always optional.
  • History is honest: renamed tables/options never rewrite old complaints (saved copies); star-ratings are never edited.
  • Fresh but stable: data reloads after 30 s idle without wiping typed text; every button shows a spinner while working so double-taps can't duplicate.
  • Scales down gracefully: paused/expired businesses show notices, disabled QRs show “unavailable” — never a raw error.
  • Light database: attendance older than 6 months is cleaned automatically; QR pictures are generated on the fly, never stored.
