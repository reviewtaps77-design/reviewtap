Complaint Lifecycle — New → In Progress → Resolved → Closed

Every spot complaint is born “new” and the owner moves it forward. Any step can be revisited.

Private Feedback Lifecycle — Unread → Read → Resolved

Simpler than complaints: read it, fix it, mark it done (or delete it).

UNREAD (automatic on submit)→ READ (owner saw it)→ RESOLVED (handled)
  • “Mark Read” moves unread → read. “Resolve” moves anything → resolved. Trash deletes it.
  • Staff star-ratings (behaviour/speed/overall) never change once saved — they are a permanent record.
  • AI review drafts are saved with the customer-edited text and a flag if they clicked through to Google.

Account, Plan & QR States — Who Sees What, When

Statuses act like switches that open or close parts of the website.

Business: ACTIVE⇄ SUSPENDED (paused by admin) EXPIRED (plan ran out)
  • Active: everything works — scans, reviews, complaints, dashboards.
  • Suspended / expired: customers see a friendly notice instead of review/complaint pages; all saved data stays safe and comes back when reactivated.
  • Staff active/inactive: inactive staff disappear from the QR list but their past ratings stay.
  • Table active/inactive: new QRs can only be made for active tables.
  • QR active/disabled: a disabled spot QR shows “currently unavailable” but old complaints remain.
  • Complaint option enabled/disabled: disabled options vanish from the customer form instantly — no reprinting.
  • Plan (monthly / 6-month / 12-month): extending adds months to the expiry date and flips the business back to active.

Attendance Cycle — Mark, View, Auto-Clean

One status per staff member per day, with a rolling 6-month window.

PRESENT ABSENT ON LEAVE
  1. Owner picks a status per person and presses Save (button spins while saving).
  2. Saving also deletes that business's records older than 6 months — history never grows forever.
  3. The calendar, last-5-day dots and 30-day totals update; the retention card shows the window start date.
  4. The purge button or the scheduled cleanup command can delete old months on demand (with a dry-run mode that only reports).
